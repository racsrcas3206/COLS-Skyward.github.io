About COLS

A day designed to build better club officials.

COLS Skyward is the Club Officials Learning Seminar hosted by the Rotaract Club of Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts & Science, parented by the Rotary Club of Coimbatore Midtown. Four masterclasses and one live workshop, led by experienced Rotaractors.
